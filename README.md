# Free100LocalLeads

[Free100LocalLeads](https://free100localleads.com) is a lead generation platform focused on US local businesses, drawing from a database of 450,000-plus contacts with phone, email, and location data. It is part of the same free-daily-leads network as [Free100Leads](https://free100leads.com).

## Claiming free local leads

Any visitor can claim up to 100 free local business contacts per day, no signup or credit card required, with the allocation resetting daily.

## Industry-specific filtering

Coverage targets local service industries such as roofing, auto repair, chiropractors, and flooring contractors, with filters for country, state, job title, and industry to build a list of a specific trade in a specific area.

## Upgrading and services

Teams needing more than the free daily batch can upgrade at [/pricing](https://free100localleads.com/pricing) (listed on-site as Upgrade). Full feature details are at [/services](https://free100localleads.com/services).

## API access

Developers can integrate the lead feed through the API documented at [/docs/api](https://free100localleads.com/docs/api).
